Built for Small-Scale Dairy Farmers

Many of the world's dairy farmers manage between two and twenty cattle — a scale that generates significant daily waste without the infrastructure to convert it into value. Terra Loop systems are engineered for exactly this context.

"Small-scale dairy farmers manage waste every day. Our systems are designed to turn that daily burden into a daily resource — clean cooking gas and organic fertiliser. The same waste that once required disposal now produces morning fuel for the kitchen and nutrition for the fields."

Benefits

What the System Delivers to Farmers

Four tangible daily outcomes from a system engineered for the small farm context.

Daily Cooking Gas

The system generates biogas on a continuous basis, providing a reliable daily source of cooking and heating fuel — reducing dependence on purchased LPG, firewood, or charcoal.

Free Organic Fertiliser

Digestate — the nutrient-rich byproduct of anaerobic digestion — serves as an effective organic fertiliser for pasture, crops, and kitchen gardens, reducing spending on synthetic inputs.

Hygienic Waste Management

The sealed system contains dung and associated odours within a managed process, improving farm hygiene and reducing the manual labour associated with daily manure handling and disposal.

Reduced Fuel Dependency

Replacing purchased fuel with on-farm biogas production reduces household expenditure and insulates farmers from fuel price volatility — an important benefit in rural and off-grid contexts.
