Our Mission

Food waste, sanitation gaps, agricultural inefficiency, and carbon emissions are not separate problems — they are symptoms of a linear economy. Terra Loop builds integrated biotechnology systems that address them as one connected challenge.

What We Build

Four Systems. One Purpose.

Purpose-built biotechnology for organic waste recovery, clean energy, and climate accountability.

Food Waste Systems

Modular anaerobic digestion systems that convert organic waste into biogas and biofertiliser — designed for communities, schools, and eco-lodges.

Learn more

Bio-toilet Solutions

Waterless sanitation systems designed for off-grid locations, converting human waste into energy — no sewage infrastructure required.

Learn more

Dairy Farmers System

Small-scale biogas units purpose-built for dairy farmers with two or more cattle — converting daily waste into daily cooking gas and fertiliser.

Learn more

Buy Carbon Credits

Transparent, verified carbon offset purchases linked to real waste-to-energy projects — with clear documentation and measurable impact.
